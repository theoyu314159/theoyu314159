## English Version
Hi! I’m Yu Lin, a high school student from the north. My life revolves around three main things: cycling, programming, and the violin. Although I bought my first road bike in elementary school, I rarely rode it until junior high, when a 50km ride with classmates ignited a true passion for cycling! I’ve upgraded from a Contend Aero to my current Scultura, and I absolutely love the thrill of the wind rushing past my ears. Programming has been a constant companion since childhood; I started out tinkering with my dad’s ThinkPad X220, moving from Scratch and HTML to Python. In junior high, I delved deeper into algorithms and data structures, fueled by my passion to compete in various informatics contests—an journey that ultimately led to my admission to my current high school through the "special talent" selection program! My current daily driver is a ThinkPad X13, and I’d love to connect with fellow ThinkPad enthusiasts. Beyond my "hacker" side, I also have an artistic streak; I joined the string orchestra in junior high and had the honor of serving as concertmaster during my second year. Practicing, competing, and performing with my fellow musicians was an incredibly exhilarating experience! This is my little programming corner—feel free to drop by, hang out, and exchange ideas!
